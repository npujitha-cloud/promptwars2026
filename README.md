NOVA RESCUE — AI-POWERED ORDER RECOVERY & INVENTORY INTELLIGENCE

PURPOSE:
Reduce NOVA CART's order cancellations, inventory inaccuracies,
delivery failures, customer support workload, and revenue loss.

CORE SYSTEM:
1. Analyze orders, inventory, delivery status, and customer behavior.
2. Identify orders at risk of cancellation.
3. Detect causes such as:
   - Product unavailable
   - Store rejection
   - Delivery delay
   - Delivery partner unavailable
4. Recommend the best recovery action.
5. Rescue the order through:
   - Nearby store switching
   - Alternative product suggestions
   - Delivery reassignment
   - Proactive customer notification

STORE INTELLIGENCE:
- Identify high-risk products.
- Detect frequently unavailable products.
- Highlight high-demand products.
- Allow stores to quickly update inventory.
- Reduce manual inventory management.

BUSINESS COMMAND CENTER:
- Orders at risk
- Orders rescued
- Cancellations prevented
- Revenue rescued
- Inventory accuracy
- Delivery performance
- Support issues

EXPECTED BUSINESS IMPACT:
- Reduce order cancellations.
- Improve inventory accuracy.
- Reduce delivery failures.
- Reduce support tickets.
- Improve customer retention.
- Protect lost revenue.
- Strengthen NOVA CART's local-store network advantage.
